HOW TO VISIT

Explore Freely

Desktop
Use the mouse to look, WASD to walk, and click a door to load the next room.
Mobile
Hold the direction pad at lower left to move and drag elsewhere to look; gyroscope controls remain available.
VR
Switch between teleportation and continuous movement in the wrist settings; grip a controller or pinch to pick up small frames.
Photos
Move close and keep looking at a photo to load the original quality.
